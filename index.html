Who We Are

Creativity with a purpose.

MR CREATIVE INDIA is a creative initiative focused on music education, media, live events and talent development. We aim to create opportunities for learners, performers and creators to learn, practice and perform.

Based in Jaunpur, Uttar Pradesh, our work brings together artistic learning and creative experiences under one vision.
